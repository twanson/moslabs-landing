AI automations for businesses that want to grow without hiring

Marketing and sales automation is no longer just for big companies. Today an SMB can reply to every lead in under five minutes, follow up on every quote and publish its content across four channels — without growing headcount. The point isn't to "use AI" because it's trendy, but to connect the tools you already have so they stop losing money through manual processes.

At MosLab we build AI automations for businesses on a simple principle: each system plugs a specific leak. An unanswered lead, a quote with no follow-up, a no-show or a customer who never returns are, each of them, money slipping away every month. That's why the six essential automations are mapped one-to-one against those leaks.

What can you automate in marketing and sales?

  • Lead capture and qualification from web, social and WhatsApp into a tidy CRM.
  • Sales follow-up on quotes with sequences that don't rely on your memory.
  • After-hours customer service with an AI WhatsApp assistant.
  • Booking, confirmation and reminder management to cut no-shows.
  • Email marketing, nurturing and reactivation of your sleeping database.
  • Repurposing your content across blog, LinkedIn, Instagram and newsletter.

Automation built for you, not templates

Every sector wastes time in different places: a clinic doesn't have the same leaks as a real-estate agency or a workshop. That's why we don't sell closed templates: we build the system around how your business actually works and on the tools you already use, without forcing you to switch platforms. The result is process automation for SMBs that fits your operation from day one.

All with a fixed price and no lock-in: you know what you pay before you start, you get documentation and training, and you decide whether to continue with a maintenance plan or keep the system running on your own. If you want to know which of the six to start with, the free 30-minute diagnosis gives you a clear plan — even if we don't end up working together.
