05Pricing FAQ

No fine print.

Does "fixed price" mean no surprises?
Exactly. We set scope and number before we start. We don't bill by the hour: if the project gets complicated on our side, that's our problem, not yours.
Is there any lock-in or mandatory fee?
No. Packages are one-off. Monthly maintenance is optional and also with no lock-in: cancel whenever you want.
What does the free diagnosis include?
30 minutes to see which leak costs you most and whether automating it will have real impact. You leave with a clear plan even if we don't work together.
Can I start with a single automation?
Yes. Any of the 6 can be built standalone from €490. Packages pay off when you want several connected together.
And the free mini-automation?
With any package you pick a free extra mini (1-2 flows, 1 integration) from a set list: lead alert, booking reminder, monthly report or welcome email.
